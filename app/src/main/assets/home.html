X5 Pro

Signals and auto bet for WinGo 30S, right inside the game.

  1. Log in and open WinGo 30S. The panel appears on the game by itself.
  2. Unlock the panel with your password and the balance you start with.
  3. Check Settings, then turn on Auto bet. Keep the app open; the screen stays on while it bets.

WinGo results are random. Losing every martingale step in a row takes the whole balance, and it gets more likely the longer you play. Use a demo account, and set a round limit in Settings.
